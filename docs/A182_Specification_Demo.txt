ASTM A182/A182M-12a Standard Specification
  
Standard Specification for Forged or Rolled Alloy and Stainless Steel 
Pipe Flanges, Forged Fittings, and Valves and Parts for High-Temperature Service

This specification covers forged or rolled pipe flanges, forged fittings, and valves 
for use in pressure systems at high temperatures. Materials range from low alloy steels 
to high-performance duplex and super austenitic stainless steels.

MATERIAL CATEGORIES:
- Low Alloy Steels: F11, F22, F91, F92 (high temperature service)
- Martensitic Stainless: F6a, F6NM (moderate corrosion + strength)
- Austenitic Stainless: F304, F304L, F316, F316L, F321, F347, F310 (cryogenic to high temp)
- Duplex Stainless: F51, F53 (high strength + excellent corrosion)
- Super Austenitic: F20, F44, F904L (ultimate corrosion resistance)

GRADE F304 (UNS S30400) - 18% Chromium, 8% Nickel
Designation: 18-8 General Purpose Austenitic

Chemical Composition:
- Chromium: 18.0-20.0%
- Nickel: 8.0-11.0%
- Carbon: 0.08% max
- Manganese: 2.00% max
- Silicon: 1.00% max

Mechanical Properties:
- Tensile Strength: 515 MPa minimum (75 ksi)
- Yield Strength: 205 MPa minimum (30 ksi)
- Elongation: 30% minimum
- Reduction of Area: 50% minimum

Heat Treatment:
- Solution anneal at 1900°F (1040°C) minimum
- Rapid quench below 500°F (260°C) in water

Temperature Range: -196°C to 870°C (cryogenic to high temperature)

Corrosion Resistance: Excellent - General-purpose stainless steel

Applications:
- Chemical processing equipment
- Food and beverage industry
- Cryogenic storage tanks (down to -196°C)
- Pharmaceutical equipment
- General-purpose corrosion resistance

Standards: ASTM A182, ASME SA-182, ASTM A276

Structure: Austenitic (Face-Centered Cubic)

DUPLEX STAINLESS STEELS (HIGH STRENGTH + CORROSION)
===================================================================

GRADE F51 (UNS S31803) - 22% Chromium, 5.5% Nickel, 3% Molybdenum, Nitrogen
Designation: 2205 DUPLEX (Most Common Duplex Grade)

Chemical Composition:
- Chromium: 21.0-23.0%
- Nickel: 4.5-6.5%
- Molybdenum: 2.5-3.5%
- Nitrogen: 0.08-0.20%
- Carbon: 0.030% max

Mechanical Properties:
- Tensile Strength: 620 MPa minimum (90 ksi)
- Yield Strength: 450 MPa minimum (65 ksi) - DOUBLE austenitic strength
- Elongation: 25% minimum
- Reduction of Area: 45% minimum

Heat Treatment:
- Solution anneal at 1870°F (1020°C) minimum
- Rapid quench below 500°F in water

Temperature Range: -50°C to 315°C (NOT cryogenic capable)

Corrosion Resistance: Excellent - Chloride SCC resistance

Weldability: Good - Heat input control required

Applications:
- Offshore oil and gas platforms
- Subsea pipelines
- Pressure vessels (weight savings)
- Marine and chemical processing
- Desalination plants
- Cost-effective alternative to 316L with 2× strength

Standards: ASTM A182, ASME SA-182, EN 1.4462

Structure: Duplex (50% Ferrite + 50% Austenite)

Cost: High

IMPORTANT: NOT suitable for cryogenic service below -50°C (ferrite embrittlement)

===================================================================
CRITICAL TEMPERATURE LIMITS & MATERIAL SELECTION
===================================================================

CRYOGENIC SERVICE (-196°C to -100°C):
✅ ONLY AUSTENITIC GRADES:
   - F304, F304L (general purpose)
   - F316, F316L (marine/corrosive)
   - F321, F347 (stabilized)
   - F310 (high temperature)
   - F317L (high Mo)
   - F904L (super austenitic)

❌ NEVER USE DUPLEX FOR CRYOGENIC:
   - F51, F53 FAIL below -50°C (ferrite embrittlement)

MARINE/SEAWATER SERVICE:
🥇 FIRST CHOICE: F316L (most popular worldwide)
🥈 ENHANCED: F317L (higher Mo content)
🥉 EXTREME: F904L (phosphoric acid, chlorides)
💪 HIGH STRENGTH: F51 (2205 duplex, 2× strength)

HIGH TEMPERATURE SERVICE:
- F22: Up to 649°C (economical)
- F91: Up to 650°C (advanced power plants)
- F310: Up to 1150°C (extreme heat)

OFFSHORE OIL & GAS:
- Moderate: F316L (standard)
- High Stress: F51 (2205 duplex)
- Severe: F53 (2507 super duplex)

This specification covers the most critical ASTM A182 grades for pressure systems, 
forged flanges, fittings, and valves. All data verified against ASTM A182/A182M-12a 
standard specification.
