### **Menu IV - Standards & Specifications**
```

☑ ASME Codes: ASME B16.5, ASME Section VIII
```

- **Reasoning:** Low carbon content prevents sensitization, excellent corrosion resistance to acids, no PWHT required

## 📊 Material Properties Quick Reference

### Low Alloy Steels (F11, F22, F91)
- **Temp Range:** 400-650°C
- **Strength:** Medium to High (415-585 MPa tensile)
- **Corrosion:** Moderate (high-temp oxidation)
- **Key Uses:** Refinery, power plants, hydrogen service

### Martensitic SS (F6a)
- **Temp Range:** Up to 400°C
- **Strength:** Medium-High (485 MPa tensile)
- **Corrosion:** Good (mild environments)
- **Key Uses:** Valves, pumps, turbine parts

### Austenitic SS (F304, F316, F321, F347)
- **Temp Range:** Cryogenic to 870°C
- **Strength:** Medium (515 MPa tensile)
- **Corrosion:** Excellent (acids, chlorides)
- **Key Uses:** Chemical, food, cryogenic, high-temp

### Duplex/Super Duplex (F51, F53)
- **Temp Range:** Ambient to 300°C
- **Strength:** Very High (620-800 MPa tensile)
- **Corrosion:** Exceptional (PREN 35-40+)
- **Key Uses:** Offshore, subsea, desalination
