ASTM A890/A890M - Standard Specification for Castings, Iron-Chromium-Nickel-Molybdenum Corrosion-Resistant, Duplex (Austenitic/Ferritic) for General Application

SCOPE
This specification covers cast duplex stainless steel (austenitic/ferritic) alloys for general applications requiring corrosion resistance and enhanced mechanical properties.

MATERIAL GRADES - CAST DUPLEX STAINLESS STEEL

GRADE 1A (CD4MCu) - UNS J93370
Composition: 25Cr-5Ni-Mo-Cu
- Chromium: 24.5-26.5%
- Nickel: 4.75-6.00%
- Molybdenum: 1.75-2.25%
- Copper: 2.75-3.25%
- Carbon: 0.04% max
- Nitrogen: 0.10-0.25%

Mechanical Properties:
- Tensile Strength: 690 MPa minimum (100 ksi)
- Yield Strength: 485 MPa minimum (70 ksi)
- Elongation: 16% minimum
- Hardness: 290 HB typical

Heat Treatment: 1900°F (1040°C) minimum, water quench or rapid cool

Applications: Marine pump impellers, valve bodies for seawater, chemical processing, paper mill components, offshore oil platforms

Structure: Duplex (30-60% Ferrite + Balance Austenite)

- Yield Strength: 415 MPa minimum (60 ksi)
- Elongation: 25% minimum
- Hardness: 290 HB max

Corrosion Resistance: Excellent resistance to chloride stress corrosion cracking (SCC) and pitting

Special Notes: This is the cast equivalent of wrought 2205 duplex stainless steel, the most widely used duplex grade in the world.

---

TEMPERATURE SERVICE LIMITS
All ASTM A890 duplex grades:
- Minimum Temperature: -50°C (-58°F)
- Maximum Temperature: 300-315°C (572-600°F)

IMPORTANT: Do NOT use duplex stainless steels below -50°C due to ferrite embrittlement. For cryogenic applications below -100°C, use austenitic stainless steels from ASTM A182 (F304, F316L, F321, F347).

---

CORROSION PERFORMANCE
Pitting Resistance Equivalent Number (PREN):
PREN = %Cr + 3.3×%Mo + 16×%N

Higher PREN = Better pitting/crevice corrosion resistance

Estimated PREN values:
- Grade 1A: ~35-38
- Grade 1B: ~37-40 (nitrogen boost)
- Grade 1C: ~38-42 (high Mo)
- Grade 4A (2205): ~35-37 (industry standard)
- Grade 5A: ~42-45 (high Mo and N)
- Grade 6A: ~40-43 (W and N enhanced)

For seawater: PREN >35 recommended
For severe marine: PREN >40 preferred

---

SELECTION GUIDELINES

Use Grade 4A (CD3MN/2205) when:
- General offshore oil and gas service
- Standard seawater exposure
- Cost-effective duplex performance needed
- Moderate stress (up to 400 MPa)

Use Grade 1A/1B (CD4MCu) when:
- Copper content enhances resistance
- Marine pumps and valves
- Brackish water service

Use Grade 5A (CE3MN) when:
- Highest strength required (515 MPa yield)
- Deep subsea high-pressure applications
- Severe corrosion + high stress combination

Use Grade 6A (CD3MWCuN) when:
- Most severe corrosive environment
- Acidic chloride solutions
- Premium performance justified
- Subsea critical components

---

MANUFACTURING NOTES
All grades require solution annealing to achieve proper duplex microstructure.
Rapid cooling (water quench) prevents detrimental phase formation.
Ferrite content must be controlled to 30-60% for optimal properties.

TESTING REQUIREMENTS
- Tensile testing per ASTM A370
- Ferrite content measurement
- Corrosion testing for critical applications
- Charpy impact testing may be specified

EQUIVALENT SPECIFICATIONS
- NACE MR0175/ISO 15156 compliant (sour service)
- EN 10283 (European standard)
- ACI designations (CD3MN, CD4MCu, CE3MN, etc.)

This specification ensures high-quality duplex stainless steel castings for demanding marine, offshore, and chemical processing applications.
