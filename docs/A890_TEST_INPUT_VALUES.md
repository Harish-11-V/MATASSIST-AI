## Input Fields Guide

### Required Fields:
- ✅ **Operating Temperature** (min-max range in °C)
- ✅ **Maximum Temperature** (peak/emergency in °C)
- ✅ **Pressure** (bar or psi)
- ✅ **Environment** (air, water, chemical, etc.)
- ✅ **Application Type** (pump, valve, vessel, etc.)

### Optional but Recommended:
- **Chemical Exposure** (specific chemicals and concentrations)
- **Required Properties** (tensile, yield, hardness, elongation)
- **Corrosion Type** (pitting, crevice, SCC, general)
- **Weldability Requirements** (yes/no, process)
- **Magnetic Properties** (magnetic ok / must be non-magnetic)
- **Certifications** (NACE, ASME, API, etc.)
- **Special Testing** (NDT, impact, SSCC, etc.)

### Advanced Options:
- **Thermal Cycling** (describe cycle conditions)
- **Fatigue Requirements** (cycles, stress amplitude)
- **Surface Finish** (Ra value, polishing requirements)
- **Cost Constraints** (budget/standard/premium)
- **Availability Timeline** (standard/expedited)
- **Fabrication Method** (sand cast, investment, machined from bar)
