## Tensile and Hardness Requirements

The following material grades are covered:
- F304 (Austenitic Stainless Steel)
- F316 (Austenitic Stainless Steel with Molybdenum)
- F310 (High Temperature Austenitic Stainless Steel)
- F321 (Titanium Stabilized Stainless Steel)
- F51 (Duplex Stainless Steel)
- F55 (Super Duplex Stainless Steel)
- F44 (Nickel Alloy - Inconel 625)
- F22 (Low Alloy Steel - 2.25Cr-1Mo)

These materials meet ASME standards for pressure vessel applications.
